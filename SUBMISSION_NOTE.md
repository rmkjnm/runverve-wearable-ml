## Wearable Fatigue Prediction Prototype

A reproducible Random Forest prototype for predicting Low, Moderate, or High fatigue from wearable-style physiological, sleep, activity, and hydration-proxy signals.

The submission demonstrates preprocessing, supervised learning, evaluation, feature interpretation, inference, and a Runverve-specific Digital Twin integration concept.

The dataset is synthetic and is used only to demonstrate the requested ML workflow.
